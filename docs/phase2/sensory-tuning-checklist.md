## Core Rule

Sound helps, but it must be refined, deliberate, and tested. Treat sound and animation as core product feel, not decoration. Do not add kid-visible preference surveys, ratings, or extra controls for this phase.

## Animation Checks

- Tempo feels smooth and slow, not sluggish.
- Trails fade gradually without cluttering the canvas too quickly.
- Particles feel responsive without creating visual noise.
- Ripples expand softly and do not dominate the screen.
- Geometric mode feels satisfying without becoming sharp or busy.
- Freeform drawing stays predictable and controllable.
- Mode transitions do not flash, jump, or resize the layout.
- Clear/reset feels quiet and non-punitive.
- Multi-touch remains stable on iPad.
- Frame rate feels stable during normal use.

## Sound Checks

- Sound starts only after a user gesture.
- Sound is easy to stop.
- Volume is conservative by default.
- Crossfades are smooth, with no hard cuts.
- Rain is soft rather than hissy.
- Ocean has slow movement and does not loop obviously.
- Drone is warm and not heavy or ominous.
- White/pink noise is not sharp or fatiguing.
- Silence remains a valid preferred state.
- Sound does not compete with the child controlling the canvas.

## Do Not Add By Default

- Reward sounds.
- Tap/click tones.
- Sudden chimes.
- Touch-reactive pitch changes.
- Unlock sounds.
- Short loops that become obvious.
- Any sound that cannot be stopped quickly.

These can become later experiments only after the ambient baseline is trusted.
